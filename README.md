# Vidya Learning Platform

Welcome to the Vidya Learning Platform, an online educational portal where learners from around the world can access a wide variety of courses taught by industry experts. 
Whether you are a beginner or a professional looking to enhance your skills, Vidya offers something for everyone.

## Features

- Expert Instructors: Learn from industry professionals with real-world experience.
- Flexible Learning: Study at your own pace with a range of self-paced and live courses.
- Certification: Earn certificates upon course completion to showcase your skills.
- Search Functionality: Easily find courses with the integrated search feature.
- Responsive Design: Optimized for various screen sizes, ensuring a great experience on both desktop and mobile devices.
